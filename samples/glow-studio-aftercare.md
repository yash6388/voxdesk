# Glow Studio Salon — Aftercare Guide

## After a keratin smoothing treatment
Do not wash your hair for 72 hours after a keratin treatment. Avoid tying your hair up, using clips
or tucking it behind your ears during this time. After 72 hours, use only sulphate-free shampoo and
conditioner. The results usually last three to five months.

## After hair colour
Wait at least 48 hours before washing coloured hair so the colour can settle. Use a colour-safe
shampoo and avoid very hot water, which fades colour faster. A hair spa every four to six weeks
keeps coloured hair healthy. Book a root touch-up every five to six weeks.

## After a facial
Avoid makeup for 12 hours after a facial and stay out of direct sunlight for the rest of the day.
Do not exfoliate or use retinol products for 48 hours. Mild redness is normal and usually fades
within a few hours.

## After waxing
Avoid hot showers, saunas and swimming pools for 24 hours after waxing. Wear loose clothing over the
waxed area and do not apply perfumed lotions on the same day.

## After a manicure or pedicure
Gel polish is fully set when you leave, but avoid soaking your hands or feet in hot water for 24
hours. Regular polish needs about two hours to dry completely.
