# Glow Studio Salon — Customer Information

Glow Studio is a hair and beauty salon at 14 Lane 5, Koregaon Park, Pune 411001.
Phone: +91 20 4000 1234. Email: hello@glowstudio.example.

## Opening Hours

- Monday: Closed
- Tuesday to Friday: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

We are closed on Diwali, Holi and Republic Day. Last appointments are taken 45 minutes before closing.

## Services and Prices

- Men's haircut: ₹500 (30 minutes)
- Kids' haircut (under 12): ₹400 (30 minutes)
- Global hair colour: from ₹3,500 (2 hours), price depends on hair length
- Highlights / balayage: from ₹5,000 (3 hours)
- Keratin smoothing treatment: ₹6,500 (3 hours)
- Hair spa: ₹1,500 (45 minutes)

### Skin and Beauty
- Classic facial: ₹1,800 (60 minutes)
- Hydra facial: ₹4,000 (75 minutes)
- Full arms waxing: ₹600 (30 minutes)

### Bridal
Bridal makeup packages start at ₹15,000 and include a trial session. Bridal bookings require a consultation at least 4 weeks before the event.

## Booking Policy

- Appointments can be booked by phone, on our website, or through our AI assistant.
- Walk-ins are welcome on weekdays, subject to availability. Weekends are appointment-only.
- Please arrive 10 minutes early. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule.

## Cancellation Policy

- Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before the appointment.
- Cancellations within 24 hours are charged 50% of the service price.
- No-shows are charged the full service price.
- Services over ₹5,000 require a 20% advance deposit, which is refundable only with 48 hours' notice.

## Payments

We accept cash, UPI, all major credit and debit cards. We do not accept cheques.
Gift cards are available in values of ₹1,000, ₹2,500 and ₹5,000 and are valid for 12 months.

## Products and Hygiene

We use L'Oréal Professionnel and Kérastase hair products, and O3+ for skin treatments.
All tools are sterilised after every client, and single-use items are never reused.
Patch tests are mandatory 48 hours before any first-time hair colour service.

## Parking and Accessibility

Free parking is available for 6 cars in the building basement. The salon is on the ground floor
and is wheelchair accessible.

## Loyalty Programme

Every ₹100 spent earns 1 Glow Point. 100 Glow Points can be redeemed for ₹500 off any service.
Members get a free hair spa on their birthday month.
