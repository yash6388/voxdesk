# Glow Studio Salon — Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Friday if a stylist is free. On Saturdays and Sundays we only take
appointments, because weekends are fully booked most weeks.

## How early should I arrive?
Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment so we can do a quick consultation. If you are more
than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the service or move you to another slot.

## Can I bring my children?
Yes. Children are welcome, and we offer kids' haircuts for under-12s. For safety, children must be
supervised by an adult while you are having a service.

## Do you do hair colour on the same day as a consultation?
For first-time colour clients we require a patch test at least 48 hours before the colour
appointment. The patch test is free and takes about 5 minutes.

## Do you sell gift cards?
Yes, gift cards are available at the reception in ₹1,000, ₹2,500 and ₹5,000 values. They can be used
for any service or product and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

## Can I choose my stylist?
Yes. You can request a specific stylist when booking. Senior stylists have a higher price for
colour services; haircut prices are the same with every stylist.

## Is there Wi-Fi?
Free Wi-Fi is available for all clients. Ask at reception for the password.

## Do you offer home services?
No, we do not offer home visits. All services are carried out at the salon in Koregaon Park.

## What languages do your staff speak?
Our team speaks English, Hindi and Marathi.

## Do you offer student discounts?
Students get 10% off haircuts from Tuesday to Thursday with a valid college ID. The discount cannot
be combined with other offers.
